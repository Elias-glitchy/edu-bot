# BUILD BOT — MASTER UX/UI DESIGN SYSTEM

Design BUILD BOT, a standalone subscription-based autonomous AI workforce SaaS platform.

BUILD BOT allows individuals and businesses to create, manage and deploy persistent AI bots that operate independently on cloud computers, execute multi-step business workflows, collaborate with other bots, communicate through text and voice, and report their progress.

BUILD BOT is independent of BUILD Growth but supports deep integration with BUILD Growth and other authorized external business software.

## Design quality
Create a premium, production-ready interface comparable in refinement to leading global enterprise software. Use a sophisticated, understated style combining clean consumer messaging interfaces with institutional-grade SaaS functionality.

Design for usability, speed, legibility, confidence and autonomy. Avoid crowded dashboards, excessive charts, colorful gradients, glowing effects and overly technical developer-tool interfaces.

## Brand and themes
Brand name: BUILD BOT.

Create every page in TWO complete themes:

LIGHT MODE:
- Main background: #FFFFFF
- Secondary background: #F5F5F5
- Cards: #FAFAFA
- Primary text: #111111
- Secondary text: #666666
- Borders: #E5E5E5

DARK MODE:
- Main background: #050505
- Secondary background: #111113
- Cards: #18181B
- Primary text: #FAFAFA
- Secondary text: #A1A1AA
- Borders: #2D2D30

Use true black and white as the foundation. Neutral grey conveys hierarchy. Reserve restrained semantic colors for success, warnings and errors when required.

Use modern typography, subtle borders, clear spacing, refined icons, rounded but not excessively pill-shaped components and accessible contrast.

Support Light, Dark and System appearance settings.

- Chief

Keep the navigation consistent across all pages. On mobile, adapt it to a compact drawer/navigation structure.

## UX principles
1. Chief is the default conversational entry point.
2. Every bot is a first-class teammate, not just an anonymous tool.
3. Use simple messaging interfaces with embedded structured widgets.
4. Allow users to inspect and control authorized cloud-computer sessions.
5. Support multi-bot group chats and handoffs.
6. Support scheduled autonomous routines and weekly missions.
7. Include text input, voice notes and live voice interaction where supported.
8. Human users remain the ultimate orchestrators.
9. Configurable approval gates protect sensitive actions.
10. Empty states must be genuine; do not prepopulate new accounts with fake missions or business results.

## Functional interaction design
Every visible interactive element needs a real intended behavior: navigation, settings, task creation, pause/resume, inspect activity, approvals, editing or filtering.

Design loading, success, error, disconnected, offline, unauthorized and empty states.

Protect against accidental financial actions or destructive operations.

Support keyboard navigation, readable responsive layouts, accessibility and consistent interaction patterns.

- Dark-mode version

Design real SaaS application screens, not promotional website landing pages.
